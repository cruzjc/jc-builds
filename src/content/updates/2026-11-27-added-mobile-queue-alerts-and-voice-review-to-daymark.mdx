The owner can opt in to alerts when a trade idea enters the queue while Daymark is open and AI advisory is running. On iPhone, add Daymark to the Home Screen and open it from its icon to enable phone alerts. Daymark shows an in-app alert when system notifications are unavailable.

Voice desk can read new queue suggestions aloud in listen-only mode. Once parked, the owner can tap to ask a question or hear a specific order ticket read back. Submitting by voice requires speaking that ticket's confirmation phrase after the readback and before the review expires. Verification used simulated orders.

The Connections page now includes troubleshooting for errors during Robinhood sign-in. A generic error on Robinhood's consent page leaves the cause unconfirmed. Robinhood directs users of unlisted platforms to its support team for help connecting.
