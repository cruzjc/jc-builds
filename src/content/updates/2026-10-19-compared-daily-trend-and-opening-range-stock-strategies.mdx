A bounded comparison tested one daily trend family and one opening-range momentum family across the same fixed stock universe. Both pooled portfolios lost under the base and higher-cost assumptions. One standalone daily trend sample remained positive under stress, but only four modeled trades and a few concentrated winners support that tentative research lead.

The retained history was already exposed, and today's stock universe does not establish historical listing or liquidity eligibility. Sparse single-venue minute bars and modeled fills further limit the results; no validated profitable strategy was established.

A small historical SIP data probe succeeded, providing a possible route for a separately declared quote-aware study. It does not establish current feed access or executable fills, and it was not substituted into the fixed single-venue comparison.

The existing fractional ETF universe, live-entry permission, independent exits and paper-strategy boundaries remain unchanged. This comparison enables no additional live or paper strategy.
