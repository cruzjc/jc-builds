The September 22 read-only performance review reconciled broker order and fill records with the internal paper ownership ledger without discrepancies. Live and paper outcomes were assessed separately, with operational health distinguished from investment results. Canonical registry validation and operational postflight checks passed.

The experimental paper strategy has begun recording fills, but the sample remains small. The review distinguished historically complete sessions from evidence that qualifies under the current source version after a code change. Earlier observations remain historical evidence; they do not automatically establish coverage or readiness for the current implementation.

Quote quality and evaluation constraints remain part of the interpretation. Complete observation coverage alone does not establish valid execution evidence, and initial paper fills do not demonstrate live profitability or readiness for promotion.

This audit made no trading or configuration changes. Detailed performance figures, account information and runtime evidence remain private.
