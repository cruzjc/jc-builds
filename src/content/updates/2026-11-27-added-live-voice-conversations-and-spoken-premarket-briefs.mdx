Tap **Start hands-free conversation** once to talk through the trade ideas in the queue and ask follow-up questions aloud. The browser streams microphone audio to OpenAI Realtime over WebRTC and plays the spoken replies as they arrive. The conversation has read-only access. To submit an order, the owner opens the parked order controls, reviews the individual ticket and confirms it.

With OpenAI connected, Daymark automatically generates a spoken summary of the premarket brief. The owner can play or replay it from the brief. The dashboard shows local time alongside US Eastern time, so the owner can check the market clock without converting time zones.
