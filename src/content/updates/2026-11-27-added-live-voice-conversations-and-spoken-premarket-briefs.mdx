Tap **Start hands-free conversation** once to talk through the trade ideas in the queue and ask follow-up questions aloud. The browser streams microphone audio to OpenAI Realtime over WebRTC and plays the spoken replies as they arrive.

During the same call, the owner can ask Daymark to review a queued order. Daymark reads the broker ticket aloud. After the readback, the owner can say the confirmation phrase for that ticket to submit it, or cancel the review before submission. Daymark reports the status returned by the broker. A submitted order can still be open or partially filled.

With OpenAI connected, Daymark automatically generates a spoken summary of the premarket brief. The owner can play or replay it from the brief. The dashboard shows local time alongside US Eastern time, so the owner can check the market clock without converting time zones.
