While parked, the owner can start a two-way voice conversation and talk through the trade ideas in the queue. The browser streams microphone audio to OpenAI Realtime over WebRTC and plays the spoken replies as they arrive. The conversation has read-only access. Submitting an order still requires reviewing and confirming an individual ticket.

With OpenAI connected, Daymark automatically generates a spoken summary of the premarket brief. The owner can play or replay it from the brief. The dashboard shows local time alongside US Eastern time, so the owner can check the market clock without converting time zones.
