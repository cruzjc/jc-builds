Internal 0.8.1 adds original material detail to the first-person sleeves and rigid arm protection in the compact solo duel. Asymmetric panels and a longitudinal seam reassign existing materials across 272 existing player faces. Mesh positions, topology, skin weights, bones, palette and collision shapes remain unchanged. This pass adds no move or enemy response.

Final source validation preserved all 21 structural and palette fingerprints and all 69 editable animation actions. The library remains 23 takes per role. Three FBX round trips passed, followed by 2,940 Unity import checks with the existing one-millimetre contact limit.

The final placement passed the existing scoped hand and skin checks, including seven support states and six complete catch routes across 2,094 dense samples. The broader rig suite passed an earlier panel placement with identical physical signatures. It was not rerun in full for the final material assignment; the scoped final checks and player captures provide the final-placement evidence.

The final Direct3D12 Windows player passed 22 open-training route witnesses, 14 solo and 8 paired, across 560 sampled states. Seven counter lessons passed across 151 states, and the original introduction passed all four reading/practice gates and both eliminations. All 334 delivery-archive files matched the packaged build. Scene and HUD captures remain separate from composited-window acceptance.

Sampled camera views show the longitudinal panel and a changing distal patch during the forearm roll. This provides a modest rotational reference at some angles, while low contrast and dark overlapping hands still hide contact. The exposed upper-arm ends, rigid glove fingers and sleeve/body continuity remain unfinished. These captures do not establish a clearly readable full turn, human motion appeal or final cinematic quality. Physical controller feel, broader balance, composited-window readability and performance still need acceptance.
