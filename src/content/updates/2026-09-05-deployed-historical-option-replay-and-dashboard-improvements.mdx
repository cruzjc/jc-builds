The LAN dashboard now serves the virtual-performance API from its single
canonical backend. Section navigation, positions near the top, explicit
Eastern timestamps, a latest-recorded-session chart selector, collection
progress, data coverage and readable job reasons make current operations and
historical simulations easier to distinguish. Research settings, evaluation
configuration and live controls are collapsed by default.

Two constrained system services now collect historical data and replay bound
option signals outside entry hours. Collection uses GET-only Alpaca calendar,
IEX bar and option-history requests, with cached immutable artifacts. The
separate replay worker has no network or access to trading credentials. Both
have resource limits and an additional market-hours guard in code.

The first on-demand collection covered 20 completed sessions, August 10 through
September 4, with all 4,680 expected IEX five-minute bars. Its fixed STO
direction observations supplied 73 sampled option signals; one missing option
reference was reported in coverage. The first replay completed and its graph
rendered without browser errors. Both installed timers subsequently fired and
completed successfully; the collector reused cached history without creating
duplicate jobs.

This is a descriptive option fill replay, not a full native strategy backtest.
It samples the first qualifying observation per symbol, direction and session
and uses explicit reference sizing. It cannot reproduce historical NBBO,
queue priority, fees, all native entry/exit state or historical account
affordability. Adaptive STO, Opening Momentum equity replay and Mean
Reversion exits still require historical adapters; their requests finish with
specific not-evaluable reasons. No result qualifies for ranking, shadow-session
evidence or live promotion.

Canonical component and platform tests passed, as did the migration suite,
registry, deployment layout audit and operational postflight. Fresh broker
and control checks verified a flat account, one dashboard writer, unchanged
revision 15 controls, Opening Momentum's blocked entries and independent exit
supervision, LAN access and public-route 404s. No broker order, cancellation,
close, trading authorization or live strategy scheduler was changed.
