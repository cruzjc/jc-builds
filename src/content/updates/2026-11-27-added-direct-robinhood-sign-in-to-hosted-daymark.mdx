From Connections in the private Daymark site, the owner can open Robinhood's sign-in page and review the requested permissions there. After returning to Daymark, the owner selects the dedicated Robinhood Agentic account in Settings.

Each direct order requires a separate confirmation in Daymark. Tickets support whole-share stock and ETF limit orders that expire at the end of the trading day. Robinhood may also ask the owner to approve the order in its app.

Robinhood returned Daymark's hosted HTTPS callback during client registration. The hosted connection opened the Robinhood sign-in page in a signed-out browser. Tests of the return to Daymark and order handling used simulated broker responses. If Robinhood declines a connection from an unlisted platform, its onboarding guide directs the owner to Support.
