Internal Windows milestone 0.9.2 adds an original layered player jacket and rounded shoulders. The existing closed sleeves remain intact beneath that layer, and the jacket follows the shared player body independently of the gun. Deterministic combat remains authoritative; world admission now also accounts for the jacket's material volume.

Source generation and exact regeneration preserve all original player vertices, sleeve caps, weights and normals, all 69 editable actions and the existing material palette. Sampled source poses, imported native-body, retained rig and synthetic input checks pass.

The Windows development build passes actual Direct3D12 room and jacket views, missed-shot return and causal failure, all 25 retained open-training routes, seven counter lessons and the original introduction. The complete delivery archive is verified against the build. Inspected views show the body and shoulders, while the rounded pads and sleeve edges remain provisional. These finite automated checks and separate scene/HUD captures do not establish human acceptance.

The first-release direction remains one compact solo arena with detailed original characters, hands, weapons and paired motion; two-opponent development is retained. Next art work is shoulder and hem refinement, articulated grasping, contact lighting and complete paired motion. Defeat clipping, elbow creasing, rigid hands and a smooth approach into first contact remain unfinished. Human playtesting, physical controller feel, normal-speed readability, composited-window presentation, packaged performance, balance and final art remain open.
