All three lap-pillow prompts place the viewer on their back, lying lengthwise along a bench and looking up from the selected character’s lap. The character sits facing forward, with their thighs at a right angle to the viewer’s body. In the head-rub variation, the character’s hands reach in at the edges of the frame to cradle and gently rub the viewer’s head, which stays out of view.

In the seated scene, the viewer sits upright with the selected character astride one of the viewer’s thighs.
