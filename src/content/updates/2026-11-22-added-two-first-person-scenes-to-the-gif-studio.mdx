The lap-pillow prompt asks for a view through the eyes of someone resting their head on the selected character’s lap. The character’s hands reach in at the edges of the frame to cradle and gently rub the viewer’s head, which stays out of view.

In the seated scene, the viewer sits upright with the selected character astride one of the viewer’s thighs.
