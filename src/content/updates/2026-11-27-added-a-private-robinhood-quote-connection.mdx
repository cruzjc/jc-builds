Daymark owners can pair their browser with a private Raspberry Pi to include Robinhood stock and options data in the desk’s market context. Daymark checks the signature on the data it receives and falls back to its existing Alpaca data when Robinhood data is unavailable.

A read-only quote request lets owners check the connection from the desk.
