The order preview shows the brokerage account, paper or live environment, quantity, limit price and any included exits. For a spread, the owner can inspect each leg before confirming the ticket. Activity provides the broker receipt and a status check for the original order.

Alpaca supports equity orders and eligible options orders, including debit spreads, in paper and live accounts. Standard Robinhood connections provide read-only holdings. Eligible accounts can submit stock and ETF orders through a separate Robinhood Agentic Trading connection in SnapTrade.
