ParryGame's 0.4.0 internal development milestone connects the next combat loop: read a threat, intercept an actual control point, redirect the weapon, sustain or release the resulting bind, recover, and take an earned or contested shot. The same vocabulary now supports solo practice and an overlapping two-opponent room.

Four threat families provide eight counter routes: a high firing line, a low firing line, a crossing weapon check and a close press. Each offers two different continuations. A clean outside redirect can earn a firing opening; another route preserves a contestable bind and another decision. Scraping contact leaves a visible raised or crossed position with an explicit recovery. Missed shots and expired control return the opponent to readable pressure, while an actual unresolved lethal line still explains a death.

Firing has a commitment interval. Enemy defensive policies are selected before the player's response and can visibly lift or block the weapon before it fires. The final authoritative muzzle decides the result. A controlled opponent does not grant immunity from the other threat, and the survivor continues attacking after the first elimination. The paired room still requires separate eliminations of both opponents.

The stop/go lessons retain unlimited reading and aiming time. Scene callouts identify the control point, redirect arrow and resulting position. Accepted actions visibly play before the next instruction holds the encounter again, giving the player time to see the resulting position. Family lessons, recovery lessons and contested-firing lessons lead into repeatable solo and paired rooms.

Original Blender work expands the library to nineteen motions across anticipation, displacement, binds, release, recovery, firing interference and defeat. The characters receive smoother dark tailored clothing, clearer armor and hand details, and more coherent silhouettes. Native limb lengths, grip relationships and contact surfaces continue to constrain the visible action. Unity presentation follows combat state; animation and IK do not decide hits.

Combat rules are frozen for each run and carry a content identity for deterministic replay. Automated combat, scene and original-rig checks passed. Synthetic input through Unity's native keyboard and gamepad paths verified both elimination orders. A separate D3D12 offscreen Windows development player check verified scene and HUD presentation across seven lessons and a two-elimination paired encounter, including survivor pressure.

Human combat playtesting, physical controller feel, composited-window presentation and packaged performance remain unaccepted. Full score attack, the launch challenge set, final cinematic content and Steam release remain later production gates.
