Three experimental units now more closely follow their concept art. Spectre gains a shaped fuselage, cockpit, and broad armor-panel seams. Citadel gains clearer twin-hull bows and bridges. Behemoth gains a more defined open-ring hull and leg guards. Faceted armor gives all three stronger silhouettes.

The models reuse shared geometry and materials without adding textures or per-unit scene objects. Each close model remains below 6,000 triangles; Spectre's distant model uses about a third fewer triangles, and the other two distant models retain or slightly reduce their budgets. Model and resource-budget checks pass.

These are geometry budgets, not a device benchmark. Frame rate on the intended laptop remains unmeasured.
