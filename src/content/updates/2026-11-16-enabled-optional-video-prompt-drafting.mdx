The hosted studio now offers optional video prompt drafting with a smaller model. You can review and edit the draft before applying it to your scene. Live checks generated a prompt for a five-second loop and confirmed that a Mixed references draft preserved the reference tag supplied with the scene. The video service reported ready.

In a separate experiment, the larger model came online after its weights were staged and warmed. Both generation requests timed out before producing a draft. During the shorter trial, the model stalled while reading a lookup table.
