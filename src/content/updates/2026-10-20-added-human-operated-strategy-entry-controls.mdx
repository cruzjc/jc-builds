A private operator page now provides reviewed enable and disable controls for two preconfigured strategies. Each change requires a fresh, short-lived review and explicit human confirmation. Source, account and risk bindings are checked again before a switch, with one entry writer allowed at a time.

Turning new entries off preserves management of existing owned positions. Strategy switches require a reconciled flat account, and failed changes retain an audit and recovery status. Existing approved limits and expiry stay fixed, while available cash can reduce the amount a strategy may use.

Validation passed 106 focused checks using temporary files, fake brokers and fake schedulers. The operator reviews the proposed change and current limits before confirming; the dashboard reports configured entry permission separately from actual execution readiness.

The software rollout preserves existing entry settings and paper-strategy boundaries; it does not activate another strategy. Historical research remains exploratory, and profitability has not been established.
