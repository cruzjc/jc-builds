The open-training encounter already moved opponents through their authoritative return paths, but static legs made those approaches slide across the floor. Delivered internal 0.5.2 adds alternating planted support and swing-foot motion while retaining the existing combat positions, commitments and timing.

The original Blender source now has a relaxed opponent stance with floor-level soles, unchanged native leg lengths and a separate editable 35 mm swing-lift curve. All nineteen paired takes remain. Unity copies the original curve and checks it against all 61 source values and derivatives.

Runtime foot placement uses the imported guard, native legs and actual weighted boot surfaces. It evaluates reach, floor clearance and separation between the boots before choosing an alternating schedule for the existing return path. The foot pose is derived from immutable movement endpoints and combat progress, so a held decision or skipped render sample can reproduce the same result. The presentation cannot stretch a leg, move the root or hips to conceal a reach failure, or change the result of combat.

A fourth complete route starts from the same shipped seed, 1042026, as the existing three. Step right after commitment to evade Opponent 1, counter and eliminate Opponent 2, then return to the centre. Evade Opponent 1's renewed shot with another right step. The survivor approaches that lane through its existing 200 mm return, then presents a close-pressure counter and earned finishing shot. This completes in order 2 then 1. The original direct-control, bind/release and spatial-evade routes retain their previous timings and scores.

The source and FBX round-trip checks passed the grounded stance and lift profile. Domain regression passed 154 checks. Unity import passed 1,674 checks, and the imported-rig suite passed 314,204 assertions, including 183,795 grounded-footwork assertions over 36 constructed art paths, 177 moving-rig samples and four complete actual encounter routes. Native synthetic keyboard/gamepad checks passed 6,682 assertions, including all four complete routes through each device path; the original controller suite passed 135 checks. Held, skipped and terminal samples preserve the measured leg, sole, hip and upper-body contact contracts.

The Windows development build completed. Its actual D3D12 open-training check passed the four routes across 106 sampled states with 212 separate scene/HUD captures, plus six lower-body inspection states with 12 additional captures. Firing feedback and a causal late-step failure also passed. The retained seven counter lessons passed 37 accepted actions across 93 states, including both firing-interference policies, attack continuation, the shared pair and survivor pressure. The original introduction passed its four reading/practice gates, anchored callouts and both eliminations. All 334 files in the delivery archive were verified against the packaged build, including its player guide.

Human playtesting must still assess cadence, weight transfer and the readability of the approaching survivor. Physical controller feel, composited-window presentation, final cinematic animation and packaged performance remain acceptance work. Broader combinations and simultaneous moving-opponent foot clearance still need validation.
