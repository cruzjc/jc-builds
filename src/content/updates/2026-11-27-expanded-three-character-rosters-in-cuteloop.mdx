CuteLoop’s character picker now has 1,670 choices across these three games. Choose a game and search for a character to preview the portrait the studio will use as a reference for your GIF.

- **Azur Lane** grew from 140 to 907 choices.
- **Girls’ Frontline** grew from 121 to 551 choices for the original game. The roster includes 458 T-Dolls, 30 Ringleader units, 50 Fairies, 11 Heavy Ordnance Corps crews, and two story characters. The T-Dolls include 40 crossover characters.
- **NIKKE** grew from 88 to 212 choices. It includes 207 collectible units and five characters playable in tutorials or events, with separate entries for alternate units and collaborations.
