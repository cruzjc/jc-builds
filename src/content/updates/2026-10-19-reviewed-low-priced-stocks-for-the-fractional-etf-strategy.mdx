A fixed screen of exchange-listed common stocks in the $1–$5 range was tested against the existing daily pullback rules. The research did not pass the chronological evaluation and higher-cost stress checks, so no low-priced-stock expansion was enabled.

Historical single-venue bars cannot establish available quotes or executable order prices. Current-universe selection, modeled fills and incomplete minute coverage keep this study exploratory; no profitable edge was established.

The existing fractional ETF strategy retains its current universe and live-entry permission. Independent exit controls and the paper strategies' live-promotion barriers remain in place.
