Pi Trade Operations now records candidate observations, scan rejections and strategy decisions as they occur. Each daily study fixes its comparison filters and source identity, while captured research preserves when it first became available. Missing, late and failed observations remain visible instead of being reconstructed with later information.

The daily review compares baseline, relative strength and VWAP, persistence, and fresh-research filters against evidence recorded before entry. Closed paper trades are linked to their original decisions and grouped by source and profile identity. Retained and vetoed comparisons reuse only observed executed trades; nontraded candidates receive no assumed returns. These descriptive comparisons do not model a replacement portfolio or establish net profitability.

A read-only dashboard panel shows collection health, coverage, filter results and the daily report. The learning records and comparison filters have no execution authority and do not change trading controls. Collection gaps, unavailable fees, paper execution limits and small samples remain explicit. Valid prospective evidence is still needed before drawing conclusions about strategy quality or promotion readiness. Detailed research, account information and performance figures remain private.
