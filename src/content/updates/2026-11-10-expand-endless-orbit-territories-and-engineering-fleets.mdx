The expansion gives Endless Orbit four times the map area, with twice the linear span. Natural strategic locations increase from 12 to 48, fleets from 24 to 96, and initial ship or wing entities from 1,152 to 4,608. Fighter and bomber wings still represent multiple craft; these counts describe simulation entities rather than independently simulated craft.

Each faction begins with a connected territory. Supplied frontier invasions reshape those territories over a campaign measured in hours. World-Architect ships now have 12,000 km hulls and construct artificial strategic positions between frontiers. Fleets can capture completed structures, siege weapons can destroy them, and surviving engineers can rebuild the ruins. Artificial locations capture faster than natural locations, allowing the front to change around durable territorial anchors.

A seeded three-hour simulation recorded 199 captures, 24 newly completed artificial locations, 55 structure destructions, and 54 rebuilds. Eighteen of the 48 natural locations ended under different ownership. All four factions retained a single connected territory at every 15-minute checkpoint. These are accelerated simulation results, not a three-hour browser endurance or target-device performance measurement.

All 10 engine tests passed. Browser checks covered the expanded initial state, controls, pause, accelerated time, save and resume, migration of an original campaign, mobile layout, engineer visuals, and the artificial-location lifecycle without console errors. Original version-1 save data is retained while the expanded campaign uses a separate version-2 save. The update was privately deployed on November 10, 2026, Hawaii time. Campaigns remain browser-local, and the Site keeps owner-only access. Target-laptop frame rates remain unmeasured.
