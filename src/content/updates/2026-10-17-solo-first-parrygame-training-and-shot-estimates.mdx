The requested training progression starts with a single opponent before introducing simultaneous threats. Delivered internal 0.5.3 adds an open solo duel as the first entry, followed by paired training. It also explains the consequences of a committed shot, including danger from the other opponent during recovery. The original guided introduction and technique lessons remain available.

The solo duel retains several legal counters, contextual steps and firing choices. Optional stop/go coaching lets the player read, aim and choose, visibly plays the accepted action, then holds the next decision. Switching coaching on or off keeps the same encounter; changing between solo and paired training starts the selected roster. No single displayed move or target becomes a required script.

Coached WATCH and deliberate waiting can play at 25%, 50% or 100%, with 50% selected initially. A visible WATCH SPEED button and fresh R or controller X / Square presses cycle the setting. Reading still stops indefinitely, and the next decision still holds after the chosen action. The setting persists across retries and roster changes. Actual combat ticks, commitments and action boundaries remain unchanged; uncoached real-time play keeps its normal clock.

The aimed Fire estimate compares the public possibilities: no interfering response, a gun lift and a barrel block. Unrevealed responses receive equal weight. A display of 33% means one of the three simulated response scenarios lands the aimed shot; it is not measured player accuracy. A visibly revealed response or a fixed teaching scenario narrows that basis. The calculation does not consult the hidden selected response to predict or weight the result, and it adds no extra random success roll. Actual position, timing and the recorded enemy response still determine play.

Landing the shot and surviving its input lock are separate estimates, with a third fraction for doing both. Causal warnings identify a known opposing impact before the bullet or during recovery only when every public scenario agrees. The assessment ends at the next legal input and cannot promise safety beyond it. A counter's anatomical annotation instead explains local weapon control, a sustained bind or compromised recovery; it does not invent a parry percentage. Early and late scraping contacts are distinguished from clean response windows.

Domain regression passed 175 checks. Native synthetic keyboard/gamepad and HUD checks passed 7,660 assertions, including six complete solo device paths and twelve complete paired device paths: three solo routes and six paired routes through each input transport. One complete solo sequence also passed at all three WATCH speeds with identical combat outcomes and event timing. Input checks passed 12 assertions, the original controller suite passed 135 checks, and imported-rig validation passed 319,806 assertions. The existing original Blender models and choreography remain the art baseline.

The Windows development build completed. Its actual D3D12 player checks passed all three solo and six paired routes across 201 main sampled states with 402 separate scene/HUD captures, plus six lower-body inspection states with 12 additional captures. The shot-risk and missed-shot probes also passed. The retained seven counter lessons passed 37 accepted actions across 93 sampled states with 186 separate scene/HUD captures. The original introduction passed its four reading/practice gates, anchored callouts and both eliminations. All 334 delivery archive files were verified against the packaged build.

The next choreography work is planned around a reachable wrist-rotation escape, followed by weapon-ownership and disarm rules, then body pivots. The requested spinning escapes and disarms are design directions, not motions demonstrated by this milestone.

Human playtesting must still establish whether the solo-first progression, alternatives and estimates are understandable in motion. Physical controller feel, broader combinations and simultaneous moving-opponent foot clearance, cinematic choreography, composited-window presentation and packaged performance remain acceptance work. This is an internal production milestone, not a finished public game release.
