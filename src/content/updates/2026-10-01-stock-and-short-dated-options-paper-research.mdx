The research now separates a stock pullback policy using completed daily bars from short-dated options hypotheses. Chronological development, validation, and held-out periods are tested with transaction costs and visible data-coverage limits. The options momentum candidates failed qualification, as did a distinct mean-reversion mechanism on earlier history. No profitable options candidate emerged, and the stock evidence remains provisional.

Rules and source identities are frozen before prospective observation. Both profiles are enrolled in the existing paper supervisor with persisted order ownership, quote validation, reconciliation, and independent protective exits, ready for the next trading session. The options profile is explicitly an execution-learning experiment, using exact next-session expirations, fresh quotes, a fixed premium limit, and intraday exits ahead of the broker's expiry-risk window. Live entries remain disabled.

The readiness review tracks implementation checks separately from trading outcomes. Paper fills, rejected opportunities, costs, and complete-session coverage will determine what can be learned from the experiment; backtests and paper results do not automatically grant live promotion.
