The September 21 daily evidence review reconciled live broker activity, the isolated paper ledger and minute-level strategy observations across six paper profiles. Live and paper results remain separate, with source and profile identity retained when interpreting evidence produced before and after code changes.

All six paper profiles recorded full minute coverage. The review distinguished valid no-trade decisions from invalid data or scan observations: complete coverage alone does not establish that every observation was valid. Indicative option pricing and a very small sample also limit what paper outcomes can establish about live execution quality or readiness for strategy promotion.

This was a read-only performance review. It made no trading or configuration changes. Detailed performance figures, account information and runtime evidence remain private.

A follow-up compared archived research available before entry with completed-bar market conditions, including nontraded candidates as controls. It distinguished research that independently aligned with observed conditions from research actually consumed by a fixed trading profile, and flagged stale or missing research and the limits of retrospective data. The findings are hypotheses for future paper evaluation, not new execution rules or evidence of live profitability. Trading controls were unchanged.
