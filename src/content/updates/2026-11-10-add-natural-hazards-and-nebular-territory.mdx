Endless Orbit now includes 12 natural hazards, with a pulsar, small black hole, and ongoing supernova in each faction quadrant. Rotating pulsar beams damage exposed hulls; black-hole gravity slows FTL charging and transit while threatening ships near the core; expanding supernova shock fronts damage fleets they cross. Fleet commanders consider hazard exposure when choosing objectives and can bend their FTL routes around dangerous regions. Environmental losses are tracked separately from combat losses.

Faction territory appears as soft nebular clouds. New campaigns scatter strategic locations into organic clusters, while existing version-2 campaigns retain their positions and ownership when upgraded. The hazard browser, observer guide, and event navigation let observers inspect these regions. The expanded campaign continues with 96 fleets and browser-local saves; earlier save versions are retained.

A seeded three-hour accelerated simulation recorded 241 captures and 898 ship or wing entities lost to environmental effects. All four factions remained connected at every 15-minute checkpoint, with 96 fleets and 4,569 surviving entities at the end. Browser checks covered all three hazard views, frozen hazard phases while paused, mobile access, and version-1/version-2 save migration with the earlier saves retained. These checks do not establish a real-time three-hour browser soak or target-laptop performance.

All 20 engine tests passed, including the three-hour campaign. The static build, controller smoke check, real-browser regression, and web-game client also passed with no browser errors. The three-hour territory view was visually reviewed to confirm that its faction clouds changed shape with ownership.

The update was privately deployed on November 10, 2026, Hawaii time, with owner-only access preserved.
