Installed a separate ETF pullback variant for an entry check at 09:36 New York time. It uses completed daily history, a fixed one-share position, one entry attempt per session, independent exits and a cash-funded limit. Research replay remains modeled evidence with an already viewed evaluation period; no profitable live strategy has been established.

The new live identity has its own source-bound controls and ownership records. Existing paper strategies retain their live-promotion barriers. Other live entries remain shut off, and the new variant remains entry-disabled while the declared whole-share purchase cannot satisfy the cash gate. No smaller position or borrowed buying power is substituted automatically.

Current Alpaca account checks follow its updated intraday-margin API rather than requiring retired pattern-day-trading fields. Every potential buy still requires fresh account, market-clock, asset and quote checks, durable order intent and reconciliation. A 5% stop is an execution trigger; overnight gaps and market execution can exceed that distance.
