The companion editor has an experimental option for turning a short scene idea into a draft video prompt. Review and edit the suggestion before applying it to the scene. Applying a draft fills the prompt field, and video generation remains a separate action.

The prototype passed local checks with synthetic inputs. Hosted deployment and live model testing are pending.
