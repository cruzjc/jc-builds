During a hands-free conversation, Daymark waits for a pause between turns before reading each newly arrived broker ticket aloud. Saying **Skip this order** leaves the ticket pending and moves on. After the full readback, submitting requires the owner to say the paper or live confirmation phrase and the four digits for that ticket.

The connected watchlist now contains only positions from the selected portfolio and symbols in the premarket brief currently on screen. A symbol appearing in both gets one row. When neither source supplies a symbol, the watchlist stays empty.

Calendar shows upcoming earnings and other dated catalysts identified in the premarket research, with links to the sources and a label for estimated or confirmed dates. It also marks exchange holidays and early closes.
