Reviewed the ParryGame combat brief on October 4, 2026. Its central direction is continuous close-quarters parrying: identify a threat line, intercept a control point, choose a redirect, and inherit a new position. The brief also calls for contestable gunfire, deterministic replay, clear death feedback, and immediate restarts.

The proposed direction now targets a finished game, with Unity under consideration. The source brief still describes a prototype, so its implementation prompt and acceptance criteria need to be reconciled with the release goal after scope decisions are made.

The next decisions cover target platforms and controls, the amount of release content, art and animation production, and the resources available to build it. This update records a planning review; implementation has not begun and no playable build is available.
