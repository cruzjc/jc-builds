Endless Front follows two autonomous factions as they build, reinforce, patrol, and bombard one continuous battlefield. Players can take first-person control of a unit and return it to the AI. Land, air, and naval units share the battle, including experimental machines with distinctive silhouettes.

The game uses shared geometry, instancing, and distance-based detail to keep large battles readable. Art improvements are checked against rendering cost; device-specific performance requires measurement on the intended hardware.
