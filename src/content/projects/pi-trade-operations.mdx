## Objective

Pi Trade Operations provides one Raspberry Pi-hosted surface for monitoring independently gated trading strategies and scheduled market research. It keeps research, deterministic strategy decisions, and broker operations visibly separate so a healthy research workflow cannot silently become order authority.

## How it works

The LAN-only `/trading/` dashboard combines scheduler health, strategy-specific controls, research artifacts, engine-applied threshold telemetry, and position or order state. Scheduled Codex research can nominate bounded equity or option candidates and recommend directional call, put, and event-risk actions. It cannot supply prices, quantities, order types, or commands.

Short-Term Options also has revision-checked strategy presets. The dashboard compares the fixed August 4 signal and session settings, the adaptive pre-change settings, and the active configuration side by side. Operators can apply either historical preset atomically or edit the supported controls individually, while the engine continues to enforce its non-configurable broker, ownership, reconciliation, affordability, and exit protections.

A three-way Directional threshold bias selector controls which side receives the easier of the profile's two entry hurdles. Prefer calls maps the lower hurdle to calls and the higher hurdle to puts, Neutral maps the stricter hurdle to both, and Prefer puts reverses the call-favoring mapping. It affects new-entry thresholds only: neither side is disabled and protective exits remain unchanged. The historical presets explicitly restore Prefer calls when selected.

For each new filled position, the engine freezes the effective entry threshold and a proportional take-profit target. Position management can also invalidate a long call or put from completed one-minute underlying bars: a confirmed adverse momentum reversal or loss of cumulative regular-session VWAP is latched before any resting take-profit cancellation. Reconciliation and a fresh position snapshot remain mandatory before the strategy submits a close, so the faster signal exit does not bypass ownership or duplicate-order protections.

Stop-loss execution has an independent, revision-checked market-close option. When enabled, a latched option stop first cancels any resting take profit or older stop, waits for broker-terminal reconciliation, re-reads the held quantity, and persists one idempotent exit intent before submitting a DAY market `sell_to_close`. Missing, malformed, or legacy controls retain the historical bid-limit behavior, and applying a historical signal preset preserves this safety setting instead of changing it.

Each deterministic engine independently reads current market and account data, validates the research schema and session window, applies configured multiplier bounds, and retains full authority over eligibility, risk, and execution. Strategies keep separate enablement, interlocks, timers, ownership state, and exit supervision; the dashboard does not collapse them into one generic live status.
