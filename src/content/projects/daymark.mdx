Daymark brings a market brief, watchlist and account balances into one workspace. Each trade idea includes its proposed entry, position size and exit conditions. For an eligible idea, the owner reviews a ticket showing the selected broker, account, quantity and price, then confirms submission. Activity records decisions and lets the owner check the order status at the broker.
