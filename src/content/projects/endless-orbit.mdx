Endless Orbit is a passive 2D canvas simulation of solar-system warfare. Four autonomous factions move FTL fleets, field colossal units, and contest evolving frontlines while an automatic observer camera follows the conflict.

The game is privately deployed through Sites. Campaigns are saved in the browser. Publication milestones and verification results are tracked in dated updates.
